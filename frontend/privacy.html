Privacy Policy

AquaFind · water refill stations in Surigao City · Last updated: November 2026

AquaFind helps people find water refill stations and see whether they have water. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you create an account or sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address and profile picture. If you sign in with email, we store your email and a securely hashed password. We also store what you do in the app: stations you save, reviews you write, and reports you submit.

How we use it

We use this information to sign you in, show your name on reviews and reports, keep your saved stations, and keep station information accurate. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

Your name and reviews may be visible to other users and to station admins. We do not share your email address with other users. Our hosting and database providers process data on our behalf to run the service.

Sign in with Facebook or Google

We only use the basic profile details you allow (name, email, profile picture). We do not post on your behalf and we do not read your friends list or messages.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your account and data at any time. See our data deletion instructions.
