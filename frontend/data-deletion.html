Data Deletion Instructions

AquaFind · water refill stations in Surigao City

You can ask us to delete your AquaFind account and the personal data linked to it, including data received from Facebook or Google sign-in.

How to request deletion

1. Send an email to sambastalipao@gmail.com with the subject AquaFind data deletion request.

2. Write the email address you used to sign in (or the name on your Facebook account) so we can find your account.

3. We will delete your account, saved stations, reviews and reports and confirm by email.

Facebook users

You can also remove AquaFind from your Facebook account at any time: Facebook Settings → Business Integrations → remove AquaFind. Then email us to delete the data we already hold.

See also our Privacy Policy.
